# Findings from the end-to-end tests

Working list, kept as the tests are written. Nothing under `src/` has been
changed for any of these. A test marked `test.fail()` with the finding's
number expects the right behaviour and fails today; it starts passing (and
the mark must then be removed) once the fix is in.

| # | Rank | What | How to see it | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Medium | After logging in from a link that carries `?redirect=`, the person lands on their dashboard, not where they were going. `src/app/login/page.tsx`: the "already logged in" effect fires as soon as sign-in sets the user and its `router.replace(dashboard)` wins over the one in `handleLogin`. So a signed-out person who opens a checkout, booking or pay link (the proxy sends them to `/login?redirect=…`) loses their place. | Signed out, open `/login?redirect=/faq`, log in: you arrive on `/dashboard/guest`. | `tests/navigation/deep-links.spec.ts` |
| F2 | High | Every press of Pay starts a new Paystack payment for the same booking. Two tabs, or a retry after a slow page, give the guest two or three payment pages, and each can be paid. The app does notice: one payment is `SUCCESS`, the others become `DUPLICATE` with a Sentry alert, and are refunded by hand. No double confirmation and no double payout happen. But the guest is really charged twice until someone refunds them. `POST /api/payments` does not reuse or refuse while a `PENDING` payment for the booking exists. | `POST /api/payments` twice for one booking, pay both at Paystack. | `tests/races/bookings.spec.ts` "pay pressed twice opens one payment page" |
| F3 | Medium | Bookings that have nothing to do with each other abort each other. `POST /api/bookings` runs in a Serializable transaction and answers every serialisation failure (`P2034`) with 409 "These dates are no longer available", without trying again. Ten guests booking ten different homes at the same instant: several are refused although their dates are free. When two guests want the same dates, about 1 time in 12 neither gets them. Nothing is oversold. | Send 10 bookings for 10 different listings together. | same file, "ten guests book ten different homes" |
| F4 | High | The sign-in limit (5 wrong passwords per address per 15 minutes, 20 per IP per hour) is counted after the password is checked. Guesses sent together all pass the count first: 12 to 18 of 20 simultaneous guesses were checked. Once the burst is over the lock does hold. An attacker who sends guesses in parallel batches gets several times the allowed rate. | 20 concurrent `POST /api/auth/signin` with wrong passwords for one address. | `tests/races/accounts.spec.ts` "twenty wrong passwords" |
| F5 | Low | The limit of 2 unpaid confirmed bookings can be passed by booking several homes at the same instant: 3 held in 1 run of 3. | 6 concurrent instant bookings on 6 listings by one guest. | same file, measured only (it is not the same every run) |
| F6 | Low | The limit of 30 messages an hour is passed by sending together: 31 to 33 of 40 were stored. | 40 concurrent `POST /api/messages`. | same file, "forty messages" |
| F7 | Medium | The sign-up limit (5 accounts per IP per hour) does nothing against simultaneous requests: 10 of 10 sign-ups sent together from one address made an account. Each one also runs a bcrypt hash, so this is also the cheapest way to load the server. | 10 concurrent `POST /api/auth/signup` from one address. | same file, "five sign-ups an hour" |
| F8 | Low | When two sign-ups with the same email or phone arrive together, the loser is answered HTTP 500 "Failed to create account", not 409 "already registered". Only one account is made (the unique index holds). | 2 concurrent sign-ups with one email. | same file, "the person who loses a simultaneous sign-up" |
| F9 | High | Calls to Paystack have no time limit. With Paystack accepting the connection and never answering, `POST /api/payments` had not answered after 20 seconds (it would wait until Vercel ends the function, 300 s by default). The same `fetch` without a timeout is used for verify, transfers and refunds, so one slow Paystack minute can hang the payout, refund and expiry jobs and every guest who presses Pay. | Make the fake hang on `initialize` for one booking and start a payment. | `tests/resilience/paystack.spec.ts` "Paystack never answers" |
| F10 | Low | When the connection to Paystack drops while a payment is being started, the guest gets HTTP 500 "Failed to initiate payment" and the `Payment` row is left `PENDING` (other failures close it as `FAILED` and answer 502). Trying again works and nothing is counted as paid. The stray `PENDING` row is asked about again by the expiry job. | Fake `reset` on `initialize`. | same file, "Paystack drops the connection" (measured) |
| F11 | Critical | A guest who cancels at the moment their payment lands can end with the booking `CANCELLED`, `paymentStatus: PAID`, a `SUCCESS` payment and **no `Refund` row**. They have paid in full, the dates are released, and nothing in the system says they are owed money: no refund is sent, no alert is raised, and the admin page has nothing to show. The cancel reads "nothing paid, so nothing to refund" while the settlement marks the booking paid; neither sees the other. In the test it happens in roughly 1 round in 5. In real use: a guest approves a slow mobile-money prompt on their phone and presses Cancel on the website while waiting. | Book an instant-book home, start a payment, mark it successful at the fake Paystack, then send `GET /api/payments/verify` and `PATCH /api/bookings/[id] {action:"cancel"}` together. | `tests/races/bookings.spec.ts` "the guest cancels at the moment the payment lands" |
| F12 | Medium | Accessibility (axe, WCAG 2.1 A and AA, 48 pages and admin tabs scanned): 63 kinds of problem on 47 pages. **Critical (13):** buttons with no name for a screen reader (icon-only buttons, 8 on 6 pages), form fields with no label (14 on 5 pages) and select boxes with no name (11 on 2 pages). **Serious (50):** text contrast below 4.5:1 on 47 pages (256 elements, mostly the muted grey and the gold on off-white), one scroll area that the keyboard cannot reach, and one definition list built wrongly on the FAQ. The full list is `e2e/.reports/a11y-*.json`; the baseline that stops it getting worse is `e2e/a11y-baseline.json`. | `node e2e/run.mjs test tests/a11y --project=chromium` | `tests/a11y/axe.spec.ts` (fails only on new problems) |
| F13 | Low | A host is sent the guest's Paystack payment reference. `bookingFor` (`src/lib/privacy.ts`) removes `gatewayReference` from the payments inside a booking, but the booking's own `paymentReference` column holds the same value and is sent to the host. The project notes say a host never gets it. | As the host, `GET /api/bookings?hostId=…` after a guest has paid: the reference is in the answer. | `tests/journeys/booking.spec.ts` "the host is not shown the guest's Paystack payment reference" |
